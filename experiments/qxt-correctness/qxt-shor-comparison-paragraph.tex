Figure~\ref{fig:qxt-shor-comparison} connects the resource score measured for
one elliptic-curve point addition with its estimated effect on the complete
28-addition Shor circuit. At the point-addition level, $Q\!\times\!T$ and
$Q\!\times\!T/p$ remain close because the observed error probabilities are
small. Nevertheless, the linearized full-circuit model amplifies the
per-addition error to $28e$, which reaches $4.704\%$ in the sampled history and
creates a more visible separation between the raw and correctness-adjusted
costs. The overall resource trend improves substantially, but the transitions
221--231, 271--281, and 391--401 illustrate that a lower raw cost does not
always imply a lower corrected cost: in each case, the resource reduction is
outweighed by the increased error penalty. These reversals motivate reporting
both metrics, since optimizing qubits and Toffoli gates alone can conceal a
regression in end-to-end reliability.
